A package written by CVB Statistics to estimate the sensitivity and
specificity of an experimental diagnostic test kit in accordance with
CVB STATWI0002 supporting the 2018 revision to VSM 800.73. See
https://www.aphis.usda.gov/aphis/ourfocus/animalhealth/veterinary-biologics/biologics-regulations-and-guidance/ct_vb_statwi
for more information.
